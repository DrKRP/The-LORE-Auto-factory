# Exam pattern: Tetralogy of Fallot (paediatrics)

Pattern entries steer coverage and ⚠ Exam notes. They never support a fact.
Frequency counts separate recall sources that showed the ask; non-PG entrance recalls are counted separately.

## P1
- ask: Components of TOF; which feature is NOT part of TOF (distractors: patent ductus arteriosus, atrial septal defect, left ventricular hypertrophy)
- frequency: repeatedly asked (seen in 4 recall sources: 2 PG-targeted, cerebellumacademy and medmastery quiz; 2 nursing-entrance recalls, AIIMS Patna NO 2020 and RUHS B.Sc Nursing 2022)
- exam_answer: Core features are RV outflow obstruction (pulmonary stenosis), large VSD, overriding aorta, RV hypertrophy. Multi-select key: A large VSD, B RVOT obstruction, C overriding aorta; LVH not a feature. "Not part of TOF" keys: patent ductus arteriosus (AIIMS Patna NO 2020); atrial septal defect (RUHS 2022)
