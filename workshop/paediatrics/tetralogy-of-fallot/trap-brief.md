# Trap brief: Tetralogy of Fallot

Sources: evidence pattern.md (P1-P4), claim-bank exam-notes K75-K80, the "Most common" table, nprep.in AIIMS recalls (boot-shaped heart 2019, squatting position 2017; snippet-level leads), and predictable wrong models. Steers design only.

## TR1
- trap: ASD, PDA or LVH is part of the four classic components of TOF
- wrong_answer: "Atrial septal defect", "Patent ductus arteriosus" or "Left ventricular hypertrophy" offered as a component; or a true component (e.g. overriding aorta, RVH) picked as the "NOT" answer
- where: "Which is NOT a part of TOF" stems; multi-select component lists
- error_type: E2
- why_tempting: ASD and PDA are common co-lesions and ride along in the pentalogy and in echo reports; LVH vs RVH differs by one letter
- frequency: 4 recall sources (cerebellumacademy, medmastery quiz, AIIMS Patna NO 2020, RUHS 2022); 2 PG-targeted
- seen_at: https://www.cerebellumacademy.com/dont-miss-these-cardiology-questions-in-neet-pg-2025-by-dr-anand-bhatia/, https://nprep.in/question-answer/which-of-the-following-is-not-a-part-of-the-tetralogy-of-2VI2GR1QBNY6LvO3Vyuo5
- refuted_by: K3, K13, K77, K9, K10, K11, K12
- yield: high

## TR2
- trap: Pulmonary stenosis is "valvular" / the obstruction is only at the valve, and RVH is the cause of the defect rather than a consequence
- wrong_answer: describes the RVOT obstruction as purely valvular, or treats RVH as a primary anatomical defect rather than secondary to the outflow obstruction and VSD
- where: anatomy-based questions; echo-report vignettes; "which component is secondary" stems
- error_type: E3
- why_tempting: "pulmonary stenosis" is the textbook shorthand; the bank says infundibular (sub-pulmonary) obstruction
- frequency: predicted (no recall shows it)
- seen_at: none
- refuted_by: K11, K12, K3
- yield: medium

## TR3
- trap: Pentalogy of Fallot adds something other than an ASD (e.g. PDA, a second VSD, or "TOF + ASD + PDA")
- wrong_answer: PDA or other lesion as the fifth component; or ASD treated as a TOF component (feeds TR1)
- where: "Pentalogy of Fallot" stems
- error_type: E2
- why_tempting: ASD and PDA are interchangeable in the learner's mind as "extra shunts"; the term is old and unfamiliar
- frequency: 2 recall sources (cerebellumacademy; RUHS 2022)
- seen_at: https://www.cerebellumacademy.com/dont-miss-these-cardiology-questions-in-neet-pg-2025-by-dr-anand-bhatia/
- refuted_by: K5, K76, K13
- yield: medium

## TR4
- trap: The boot-shaped heart is present in most infants with TOF, and is the radiograph to expect; or its cause is the left ventricle / a large pulmonary artery
- wrong_answer: "Boot-shaped heart" as the always-present finding; attributing the upturned apex to LVH; expecting plethoric lungs
- where: "Boot-shaped heart is seen in" recalls (AIIMS Manglagiri 2019 per snippet); X-ray vignettes
- error_type: E4
- why_tempting: boot-shaped heart = TOF is a one-line key; qualifiers ("may", "classically", not in most infants) get dropped
- frequency: 2 recall sources (cerebellumacademy P3; nprep AIIMS 2019 snippet-level)
- seen_at: https://www.cerebellumacademy.com/dont-miss-these-cardiology-questions-in-neet-pg-2025-by-dr-anand-bhatia/, https://nprep.in/question-answer/in-which-disease-condition-a-boot-shaped-heart-is-seen-in-chest-nqnlwte_HDi9rVRUqt71M
- refuted_by: K78, K4, K32, K31, K21
- yield: high

## TR5
- trap: Confusing the TOF radiograph with other cyanotic CHD signs (egg-on-a-string for TGA, snowman for supracardiac TAPVR) or expecting increased pulmonary vascularity
- wrong_answer: assigns egg-on-a-string or snowman to TOF, or boot shape to TGA / tricuspid atresia
- where: "Sign - disease" matching; "5 T's" cyanotic CHD stems; radiology INI-CET
- error_type: E10
- why_tempting: the 5 T's all start with T and share cyanosis; sign names are memorised as a list
- frequency: 2 search snippets (nprep AIIMS 2019 boot-shaped; getoncourse CHD imaging)
- seen_at: https://nprep.in/question-answer/in-which-disease-condition-a-boot-shaped-heart-is-seen-in-chest-nqnlwte_HDi9rVRUqt71M, https://getoncourse.ai/revision/indian-medical-pg/radiology/cardiovascular-radiology/congenital-heart-disease/
- refuted_by: K4, K21, K31
- yield: medium

## TR7

- error_type: E4
- why_tempting: "cyanotic heart disease" label; source tension (K24 presents as neonate vs PYQ "beyond neonatal")
- frequency: search-snippet lead only (pink vs blue tet)
- seen_at: none

## TR8
- trap: During a tet spell the murmur gets louder (or the murmur gets louder with more cyanosis)
- wrong_answer: "Murmur intensity increases during the spell"; or "the more severe the TOF, the louder the murmur"
- where: spell vignettes; "which finding fits a hypercyanotic spell"
- error_type: E3
- why_tempting: general rule is a more severe stenosis gives a louder murmur; in a spell the murmur softens as flow across the RVOT falls
- frequency: predicted; search snippet shows learners reverse squatting vs spell murmur change
- seen_at: https://wikem.org/wiki/Tet_spell
- refuted_by: K29, K26, K20
- yield: high

## TR9
- trap: Squatting and knee-chest work by lowering pulmonary resistance or raising pulmonary flow directly; the mechanism is wrongly stated as increased "peripheral" or pulmonary resistance
- wrong_answer: "decreases pulmonary vascular resistance" / "increases PVR"; "venous return from legs increases and relieves RVOT"
- where: "Mechanism of squatting in TOF"; AIIMS Rishikesh/Jodhpur 2017 "common position in a child with TOF" (squatting)
- error_type: E3
- why_tempting: PVR and SVR are one letter apart; learners recall that squatting improves lung flow but not why
- frequency: 1 recall source (nprep AIIMS Rishikesh & Jodhpur NO 2017, snippet-level)
- seen_at: https://nprep.in/question-answer/common-position-in-a-child-with-tetralogy-of-fallot-7_dvd1YS8Z8QYPsIgwxMi
- refuted_by: K43, K47
- yield: high

## TR10
- trap: First step in a tet spell is drugs (morphine, propranolol, phenylephrine) or intubation; positioning and oxygen are skipped; or a diuretic/inotrope is chosen
- wrong_answer: IV propranolol / morphine / sodium bicarbonate chosen first; digoxin or furosemide chosen
- where: "Next best step in a cyanotic spell" stems
- error_type: E9
- why_tempting: drugs feel more active than positioning; the list of treatments is long and unordered
- frequency: predicted (no recall in pattern.md); conceptually central
- seen_at: none
- refuted_by: K42, K43, K44, K45, K46, K47, K48
- yield: high

## TR11
- trap: Hypercyanotic spells are best treated by an inotrope or by raising pulmonary flow with a vasodilator; fluid is withheld because it is a "heart" problem
- wrong_answer: digoxin, diuretics or avoiding fluids; or giving nitroprusside to reduce afterload
- where: management MCQs
- error_type: E3
- why_tempting: heart disease reflexes (diuretic, digoxin) apply to heart failure, not to dynamic RVOT spasm; the aim is to raise systemic afterload and fill the RV
- frequency: predicted
- seen_at: none
- refuted_by: K45, K46, K47, K44
- yield: medium

## TR14

## TR15
- trap: Pulmonary component of S2 is loud, or the S2 is widely split, in TOF
- wrong_answer: loud P2 / wide split S2 / ejection click confusions
- where: auscultation stems; look-alike with pulmonary hypertension or ASD
- error_type: E1
- why_tempting: learners associate "pulmonary" with loud P2; the real finding is an inaudible P2
- frequency: predicted
- seen_at: none
- refuted_by: K27, K25

## TR16
- trap: 22q11.2 deletion is present in most TOF cases, or the commonest association is Down syndrome / Turner / another syndrome; and exact percentages are mixed up
- wrong_answer: Down syndrome (AV canal) as the usual TOF association; or 22q11.2 deletion in over half; 6% vs 15% swapped
- where: "Genetic association of TOF"; DiGeorge look-alike stems
- error_type: E10
- why_tempting: Down syndrome is the best-known CHD syndrome but is linked to AVSD; DiGeorge vs VCFS naming
