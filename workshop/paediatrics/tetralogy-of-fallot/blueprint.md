# Blueprint: Tetralogy of Fallot

**Summary for the learner:** Why one outflow obstruction explains the blue baby, the murmur, the spells, the boot and the surgery.

## Why this order

Standard journey, 8 segments, with clinical and diagnosis split because TOF has two distinct bodies of exam-tested findings (bedside signs vs investigations), and management carrying both the acute spell and the long-term surgical story.

1. **S1 hook** – a blue infant with a harsh murmur: name it and wonder why.
2. **S2 retrieve** – the four components (no published paediatrics modules yet, so retrieval is from anatomy/prior study). Retrieval gate with a fast lane for those who know them; P1 distractors (ASD, PDA, LVH) are the wrong options.
3. **S3 mechanism** – "it's the outflow, not the hole". The single idea everything else hangs on: RVOT obstruction decides how much blood bypasses the lungs.
4. **S4 clinical** – murmur, S2 and spell triggers derived from the mechanism; the murmur softening during a spell is the key predict-then-explain moment.
5. **S5 diagnosis** – ECG, boot-shaped heart (normal-size silhouette, upturned apex from RVH, concave PA segment), echo as gold standard.
6. **S6 management** – case-led tet spell (knee-chest, O2, fluids, beta-blocker, phenylephrine, morphine), then repair timing, BT shunt, post-repair and unrepaired outcomes.
7. **S7 traps** – P1–P4 drilled, with both-key teaching.
8. **S8 consolidate** – learner-built one-pager and review cards.

## Misconceptions that matter most

- **M1–M3 (ASD / PDA / LVH as a component)** – the most repeatedly asked PYQ (P1, 4 recall sources). Hit in S2 and again in S7 for a delayed re-test.
- **M5 (cyanosis comes from the VSD)** and **M6 (murmur from the VSD, louder in a spell)** – the wrong mental model that makes the spell physiology unlearnable. S3 and S4 target them directly.
- **M7 (vasodilate / diurese in a spell)** – the dangerous management error; S6 case, re-tested in S8 ordering.
- **M8/M9 (boot = cardiomegaly; CXR is diagnostic)** – the P3 key invites both.
- **M4 (pentalogy adds a PDA)** and **M10 (reoperation for residual VSD)** – smaller but exam-relevant.

## Where the unverified claims go

- **K75 / K2 / K70** ("most common cyanotic CHD beyond the neonatal period"): S7 exam-trap node only, flagged ⚠, taught as "PYQs have expected X; current StatPearls says TOF most commonly presents as a cyanotic neonate (K24)".
- **K78 / K33** (boot not seen in most infants): S7 trap and S5 `deeper`, flagged.
- **K7, K17, K18, K71** (22q11.2) and **K15, K16** (other incidence figures): S7 `deeper`, flagged; the verified K79/K80 carry the "sources differ" message.
- **K51–K58** (repair window, staged palliation, shunt drawbacks) and **K36, K38, K40, K41** (imaging limits): S6 / S5 `deeper` branches only, flagged. Never on the core path.
