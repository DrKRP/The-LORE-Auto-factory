## Why this order and these departures

- **No separate retrieve segment.** No paediatrics modules are published yet, so nothing can be bridged. The hook's first probe (P12, most common cyanotic CHD) does the job of activating prior knowledge, and authors can use a retrieval gate there.
- **The clinical picture comes before investigations.** S3 sets up the K22 balance (RVOT against systemic resistance) and the softening spell murmur. S5's spell management builds on both, and S4's oligaemic film reuses K20 from S2.
- **There are two management segments.** Spell management (a bedside emergency, priority errors) and surgery plus long-term care (timing qualifiers, eponyms, superlatives) carry different trap families. One segment would have held ten probes.
- S7 is heavy (11 probes). Low-yield re-tests (P32, P34, P44) can sit in deeper branches. Authors should keep the high-yield ones on the main path: P07, P09, P10, P12b and P13.

## Backbone

Three wrong models drive most of the traps, as the trap map's `probe_plan` describes:
1. **"TOF is a list of shunts with a left-sided picture"** (TR1, TR2, TR3, TR14). S2 repairs it and S4 and S8 re-test it.
2. **"Cyanosis is fixed by the anatomy"** rather than set by the RVOT-versus-SVR balance (TR7–TR11). S3 sets it up, S5 applies it and S8 re-tests it.
3. **Stretched superlatives and dropped qualifiers** (TR6, TR4, TR7, TR18, TR20, TR21). Each is surfaced early and re-tested in S7, S8 or both.

Every trap appears in at least two segments. TR16 and TR17 first appear in S2's deeper "associations" branch and come back in S7.

## Unverified claims

Unverified claims never sit on the core path. They are grouped under O12 (the non-core "PYQ key versus current source" objective) and in O11 (associations: K7, K17, K18, K71, K15, K16). They appear only in:
- S7 exam-trap nodes with ⚠: K2, K70 and K75 (age qualifier); K78 and K33 (boot shape); K53, K55, K56 and K57 (palliation); K7, K17, K18 and K71 (22q11.2).
- Deeper branches in S2 (associations), S4 (K36, K38, K40, K41: imaging limits) and S6 (K51, K52, K58: timing nuance).

Core objectives cite only verified claims. All verified high-yield claims are under a core objective: K1 and K69 under O3, K3 and K5 under O1, K4 under O5, K6 and K72 under O6, K73 under O9, and K74 under O10.

## Guardrails for authors (from the trap map's Unrefutable list)

The feedback must never assert:
- that TGA is the commonest cyanotic CHD in neonates
- which lesion has the egg-on-a-string or snowman sign
- the mechanism of squatting
- the Waterston, Potts or Glenn connections
- the Down syndrome–AVSD link

P23 refutes furosemide by the aim of each treatment, not by a "contraindicated" claim.
