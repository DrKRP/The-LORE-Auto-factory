# Trap brief: Thyroid nodule and differentiated thyroid cancer

## TR1
- trap: FNAC is the first investigation for a solitary thyroid nodule
- wrong_answer: "Ultrasound-guided FNAC" as the first test for a nodule
- where: P2 "first investigation" stems; "investigation of choice" stems read as "first"
- error_type: E5
- why_tempting: the older PYQ key says FNAC; "investigation of choice" is the same phrase used for FNAC in the classic table
- frequency: model knowledge (P2)
- refuted_by: K125, K54, K134, K4
- yield: high

## TR2
- trap: A radionuclide scan is the first step for every nodule
- wrong_answer: "Radioiodine uptake scan" offered as the first investigation
- where: nodule work-up stems; "which investigation first" stems
- error_type: E8
- why_tempting: the scan is the classic test of nodule function and sits high in the work-up list
- frequency: model knowledge
- refuted_by: K4, K55, K56
- yield: medium

## TR3
- trap: Follicular carcinoma is the most common thyroid cancer
- wrong_answer: "Follicular carcinoma" when the stem names iodine deficiency
- where: "most common" stems with a geographic or dietary clue
- error_type: E7
- why_tempting: papillary is the default answer, so the learner stops reading at "most common"; the iodine-deficient qualifier is what flips it
- frequency: model knowledge
- refuted_by: K2, K121, K23, K118
- yield: medium

## TR4
- trap: Cytology or frozen section can diagnose follicular carcinoma
- wrong_answer: "FNAC" or "frozen section" offered as the test that makes the diagnosis of follicular carcinoma
- where: P4 "why FNAC cannot diagnose follicular carcinoma"; Bethesda IV stems
- error_type: E8
- why_tempting: FNAC diagnoses papillary carcinoma by nuclear features, so the learner carries the same test to follicular lesions
- frequency: model knowledge (P4)
- refuted_by: K6, K38, K65, K66, K127
- yield: high

## Unrefutable
- Hashimoto thyroiditis is a risk factor for papillary carcinoma (associated, not in the bank; K24 covers only lymphoma).
- Anaplastic carcinoma responds to radioiodine as DTC does (no claim addresses its radioiodine response; K14 says only that it is not DTC).
- Primary thyroid lymphoma is managed with radioiodine (no claim on lymphoma treatment).
