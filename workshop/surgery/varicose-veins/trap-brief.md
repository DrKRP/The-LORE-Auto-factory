# Trap brief: Varicose veins

Sources: `evidence/surgery/varicose-veins/pattern.md` (P1–P14, model-knowledge asks), the note's ⚠ Exam notes (K119–K126, all PYQ-key vs current-practice or confusable-test conflicts), the note's "Most common / Investigation of choice / Drug of choice" table (K105–K118), and knowledge of NEET PG / INI-CET stems. Frequencies are pattern-only (no recall sources were available in knowledge mode). Each trap names a wrong model; `refuted_by` lists the claim-bank IDs that show it wrong.

## TR1
- trap: Trendelenburg operation with stripping is the first-line treatment for SFJ incompetence
- wrong_answer: "Trendelenburg operation + stripping" offered as the first-line current treatment of truncal reflux
- where: "Treatment of choice for varicose veins with SFJ incompetence / first-line current treatment" stems (P4); options that list ablation next to the old surgery
- error_type: E5
- why_tempting: The older PYQ key and most textbook chapters still describe the Trendelenburg operation as the classic answer; the learner memorised the key, not the current order
- frequency: P4 (pattern-only)
- seen_at: model knowledge
- refuted_by: K5, K72, K110, K119, K111
- yield: high

## TR2

## TR4

- error_type: E10
- why_tempting: Both are tourniquet tests named after clinicians, both are used in the same consultation, and the names are easily swapped

- seen_at: model knowledge
- refuted_by: K4, K113, K114, K120
- yield: high

## Unrefutable
- Smoking or alcohol as a varicose vein risk factor: not in the note, so no claim can confirm or refute it.
- Exact length of the GSV in centimetres: no number in the note.
- Arterial ulcer features (sites, pulses, pain pattern) beyond the ABPI threshold: the note does not describe arterial ulcers.
- Pregnancy-related varicose veins resolving after delivery: the note does not give the course after delivery.
