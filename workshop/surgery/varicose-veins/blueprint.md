# Blueprint: surgery/varicose-veins

Standard journey with two changes: no separate retrieve segment (no published venous or vascular module to bridge from; retrieval is folded into the S1 hook gate), and the clinical picture comes before bedside tests and treatment is split in two (ladder, then safe operating and the ulcer leg).

12 objectives (11 core). All 24 verified high-yield claims (K1-K10, K105-K118) sit under core objectives. Only O12 (varix vs hernia) is non-core.

## Segments
- S1 hook (T9): commonest-of-everything stem; TR19, TR7 first probes.
- S2 mechanism: flow direction, calf pump, pressure; anatomy and named perforators (TR11, TR12, TR10, TR7). These are the backbone traps everything later builds on.
- S3 clinical (T2): ulcer site, skin, CEAP, varix vs hernia (TR31, TR14, TR24).
- S4 diagnosis (T5): Brodie-Trendelenburg vs Perthes, duplex, deep-vein check (TR2, TR4, TR5, TR6, TR18). The mechanism probe sits before the fork.
- S5 management (T11): ladder, PYQ key vs current practice, stockings, sclerotherapy (TR1, TR17, TR21, TR22).
- S6 management (T5): stripping extent, nerves, deep-vein precondition, ABPI before compression (TR9, TR8, TR18, TR16, TR17, TR31).
- S7 traps (T10): which-is-not and look-alike closers (TR2, TR4, TR19, TR24, TR10, TR6, TR14, TR22, TR5, TR21).
- S8 consolidate: short mixed quiz of 8-10 NEW stems (playbook), at least one cold probe, no new teaching.
