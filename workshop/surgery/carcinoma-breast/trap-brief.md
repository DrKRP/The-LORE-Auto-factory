# Trap brief: Carcinoma breast

Sources: note ⚠ Exam notes, the "Most common / Investigation of choice / Drug of choice" table, `evidence/surgery/carcinoma-breast/pattern.md` (P1–P14), and model knowledge of NEET PG / INI-CET stems. Every `refuted_by` ID is a claim in `claim-bank.json`.

## TR1
- trap: FNAC is the investigation of choice for a breast lump
- wrong_answer: "FNAC" offered as the investigation of choice for tissue diagnosis of a breast lump
- where: P3 (triple assessment, investigation of choice); "Investigation of choice for tissue diagnosis" stems; older keys
- error_type: E5
- why_tempting: FNAC was the classic answer in older keys and is still used for axillary nodes and cysts (K56), so the learner's memory holds "FNAC" as the breast test
- frequency: pattern P3 (older keys); model knowledge
- seen_at: model knowledge
- refuted_by: K129, K137, K54, K56
- yield: high
