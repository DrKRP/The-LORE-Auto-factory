# Blueprint: Trauma primary survey (ATLS)

11 objectives (10 core), 20 traps, 7 segments. Standard journey with the traps segment folded in: each system segment (B, C, resuscitation) already carries its own distractor drills, and the final segment is a short mixed quiz of new stems (playbook).

1. S1 hook (T9): learner fails a bleeding-patient "first step" stem. Backbone TR1, TR2.
2. S2 retrieve: trimodal death peaks bridged to time-to-death; TR30 and the MARCH conflict (K133, unverified, shown with warning).
3. S3 mechanism: airway, spinal motion restriction, GCS 8, adult vs child surgical airway (TR2, TR22).
4. S4 clinical (T7 preferred): breathing cluster, TR3 first because TR5, TR7, TR8 offer it as a distractor; TR7 is the hub; also TR16, TR35, TR14, TR11.
5. S5 diagnosis (T5 preferred): compensated shock, Class III, shock types, FAST not CT (TR6, TR9, TR17, TR4, TR23). Mechanism probe before the fork.
6. S6 management (T11 preferred): fluids, TXA, permissive hypotension vs TBI, urethrogram, old-key vs ATLS conflicts (TR10, TR18, TR27, TR11, TR22, TR23, TR14).
7. S7 consolidate (T9): new-stem mixed quiz, skip lane, re-tests every trap cold.

Unverified claims (K21, K42, K70, K78, K131, K132, K133, K134) appear only as warnings or in non-core objective O11. All verified high-yield claims sit under core objectives O1-O10.
