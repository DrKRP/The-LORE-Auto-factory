# Blueprint: Perforated Peptic Ulcer

Journey: hook (S1) -> mechanism (S2) -> clinical (S3) -> two diagnosis segments (S4 free gas signs, S5 test ladder) -> management (S6) -> exam traps (S7) -> mixed quiz of new stems (S8).

Departures from the standard journey: no retrieve segment (no published surgery modules to bridge from); diagnosis is split in two because TR3/TR4/TR5/TR6/TR7/TR8/TR9/TR20 are many and co-trap in pairs (free-gas reading, then first vs best).

Backbone traps: TR1 (wall to outcome), TR5/TR6 (first vs best, PYQ vs current), TR7 (clear film), TR11 (resuscitate first), TR12 (Taylor qualifier), TR17 (false-calm reaction). TR7, TR23, TR17 get their cold re-test in S8.

Unverified claims (K35, K53, K56) are non-core and sit only in S3/S5/S6 as side detail; K53 and K56 are optional colour under O10.

Final segment S8 is a short mixed quiz of new stems with a skip-if-correct lane (playbook consolidate lesson), not a recap.

Technique hints: S1 T9, S2 T7, S3 T2, S4 T5, S5 T7, S6 T5, S7 T10, S8 T9 (from technique-stats and playbook).
