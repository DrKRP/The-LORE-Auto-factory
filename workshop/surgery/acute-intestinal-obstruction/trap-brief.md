Basis: the note's ⚠ Exam notes (K143–K150), the High-yield and Most-common tables (K125–K142), and model knowledge of NEET PG / INI-CET stems. No `evidence/surgery/acute-intestinal-obstruction/pattern.md` exists, so `frequency` below is an estimate from knowledge, not a count of recalled stems. `seen_at` is none (knowledge mode).
