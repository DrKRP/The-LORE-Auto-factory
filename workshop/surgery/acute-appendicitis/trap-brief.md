# Trap brief: Acute appendicitis

Built from knowledge mode: pattern.md P1–P12, the note's ⚠ Exam notes (K106–K113), and the High-yield / Most-common table (K91–K105). Every trap names the claim-bank IDs that show it wrong. Verified claims are listed first.

## TR1
- trap: Psoas and obturator manoeuvres are swapped (psoas elicited by internal rotation of the flexed hip; obturator elicited by hip extension)
- wrong_answer: "Pain on extending the right hip = obturator sign" / "pain on internal rotation of the flexed hip = psoas sign"
- where: P2 stems ("which is elicited how"); sign-matching MCQs; vignettes describing an examiner's manoeuvre
- error_type: E1
- why_tempting: Both are right-hip manoeuvres that produce RIF pain; the names sound interchangeable and the student remembers "hip" but not the movement
- frequency: P2 (model knowledge); high-frequency PG sign question
- seen_at: model knowledge
- refuted_by: K38, K39, K106
- yield: high

## TR2
- trap: Each sign is tied to the wrong appendix position (psoas read as pelvic; obturator read as retrocaecal; rectal tenderness read as retrocaecal)
- wrong_answer: "Psoas sign suggests a pelvic appendix" / "Obturator sign suggests a retrocaecal appendix" / "Right-sided rectal tenderness suggests retrocaecal"
- where: P2 ("which appendix position each suggests"); position-to-sign matching; vignettes with a positive sign and a named position in the options
- error_type: E10
- why_tempting: The student memorises a sign and a position as a pair but attaches the pair to the wrong position, because the retrocaecal and pelvic positions are the two commonest and get swapped
- frequency: P2 (model knowledge)
- seen_at: model knowledge
- refuted_by: K38, K39, K42, K106, K92, K93
- yield: high

## TR4
- trap: Pain pathway reversed: RIF pain is taken as the early visceral pain and periumbilical pain as the later somatic pain
- wrong_answer: "Initial pain localised to the RIF, later spreading to the umbilicus, because the parietal peritoneum is involved first"
- where: Clinical-features stems ("early" vs "late" pain site); pathophysiology MCQs on the cause of migration
- error_type: E3
- why_tempting: The final picture (localised RIF tenderness) is memorised; the student runs the causal chain backwards from the end-point
- frequency: high-yield classic (K5, K26, K27); no separate PYQ row, but underlies the migration sign
- seen_at: model knowledge
- refuted_by: K5, K26, K27, K30
- yield: high

## TR5
- trap: Symptom order reversed: vomiting taken as coming before pain in the typical sequence
- wrong_answer: "Vomiting followed by pain, then fever, is the typical appendicitis sequence"
- where: Murphy's triad / sequence stems; "which symptom comes first" distractors
- error_type: E9
- why_tempting: "Vomiting first" fits gastroenteritis and intestinal obstruction, which students meet in the same chapter; the sequence is memorised as a list with no order
- frequency: model knowledge (K6)
- seen_at: model knowledge
- refuted_by: K6, K31
- yield: medium

## TR6
- trap: Alvarado weighting wrong: migration or rebound tenderness scored 2 points
- wrong_answer: "Migration of pain to RIF = 2" or "Rebound tenderness = 2"
- where: P3 ("components and weighting"); score-item completion MCQs; total-score arithmetic vignettes
- error_type: E6
- why_tempting: Rebound and migration feel like the most specific signs, so the student gives them the higher weight; tenderness and leucocytosis are the two 2-point items and the student only remembers one
- frequency: P3 (model knowledge); K107 flags it as the trap option
- seen_at: model knowledge
- refuted_by: K7, K107
- yield: high
