# Blueprint: Inguinal hernia (6 segments)

Objectives: 11 (8 core). Traps: 20 (all from trap-map.json). All 24 high-yield claims sit under core objectives; every claim is verified.

Order follows the trap map's probe_plan. Backbone: TR1 (route against the inferior epigastric vessels) opens in the hook and returns in S2 and S3, because TR40 and TR21/TR22/TR23 all reuse that position rule. TR8/TR9/TR5 share the "qualifier changes the superlative" skill, so they sit together in S4. TR7/TR16/TR17/TR39 share options, so they sit together in S5.

- S1 hook: fail a direct-vs-indirect stem and a "most common in females" stem (TR1, TR40, TR5).
- S2 mechanism: canal walls, three-sided Hesselbach triangle, congenital vs acquired (TR2, TR28, TR31); prefers T6 refutation.
- S3 clinical: shape, descent, occlusion and Zieman tests (TR23, TR21, TR22).
- S4 diagnosis: strangulation risk, groin vs inguinal wording, investigation level, taxis, appendix eponyms (TR8, TR9, TR12, TR11, TR34, TR5 retest).
- S5 management: operation per patient, nerve and triangle hazards (TR7, TR16, TR17, TR39, TR14, TR25).
- S6 consolidate: cold retests in new forms and learner-built summary.

Departures: no retrieve segment, because there are no published surgery modules to bridge from and the length cap is 6; the hook carries the activation. Exam-trap segment folded into S5 and S6 to stay at 6; the exam-key notes (K108, K109, K110, K111, K112) are taught on the correct path where each trap lives. O4, O5, O11 are non-core (medium exam weight, no high-yield claim).
