# Trap brief: Burns: assessment and fluid resuscitation

Basis: `pattern.md` P1–P12, the note's ⚠ Exam notes (K82–K87, each an E5/E7/E8 candidate), the note's Most common table (K68–K81), and knowledge-mode recall of NEET PG / INI-CET burn stems and their distractors. All evidence is model knowledge; refutations point to claim-bank IDs.

## TR1
- trap: Parkland 4 mL/kg/%TBSA is the answer to every fluid question
- wrong_answer: "4 mL x kg x %TBSA" offered as the current starting rate, with no titration or lower start
- where: P1 and fluid-volume stems; "best initial fluid" stems; "fluid creep" wording
- error_type: E5
- why_tempting: PYQ keys have always expected 4 mL; it is the only formula most students memorised
- frequency: P1 (pattern); high-frequency exam key
- seen_at: model knowledge
- refuted_by: K82, K47
- yield: high

## TR2
- trap: The 8-hour half of the Parkland volume is counted from arrival at hospital
- wrong_answer: "First 8 h from the time of arrival / admission"
- where: P2 numeric vignettes giving a time of burn and a later arrival time
- error_type: E9
- why_tempting: the clinical clock starts when the patient reaches the team; the burn time is easy to skip in the stem
- frequency: P2 (pattern); recurring numeric key
- seen_at: model knowledge
- refuted_by: K83, K43
- yield: high
