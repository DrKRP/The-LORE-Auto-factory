# Blueprint: Burns assessment and fluid resuscitation

Order follows the trap map's probe plan: safety first (S1-S2), diagnosis of shock and depth (S3), measurement (S4), the Parkland chain (S5), titration and procedures (S6), exam-trap corrections (S7), cold consolidation (S8).

- Backbone traps: TR29/TR30 (airway first), TR13/TR12 (inhalation ladder), TR1 then TR2-TR4 (Parkland chain), TR16/TR17 (titration).
- Departure from the standard journey: no separate mechanism segment. The burn-shock mechanism is short (K17-K19) and folded into S3 as a clinical segment so it sets up the fluid probes.
- Unverified claims K47 and K48 (current ATLS 2 mL rate, rule of tens) sit in S5 and S7 only, shown with a caution flag; classic key (4 mL, K82) is taught first, both columns stated.
- S6 routes to S8 as a fast lane for learners who pass the titration probes.
- Technique hints: T9 for S1 and S8 (playbook wins), T7 for S4, T5 for S5, T6 for S7.
