## P1
- ask: Parkland formula for fluid resuscitation in burns (volume, fluid, timing)
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: 4 mL x kg x %TBSA of Ringer lactate in 24 h; half in first 8 h
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P2
- ask: Calculate fluid in the first 8 h for a given weight and %TBSA (numeric vignette)
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Total by Parkland divided by 2, counted from time of burn
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P3
- ask: Rule of nines values (adult anterior trunk, each lower limb, head, perineum)
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Head 9, arm 9, trunk front 18, back 18, leg 18, perineum 1
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P4
- ask: Best chart for TBSA in children
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Lund and Browder chart
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P5
- ask: Best indicator of adequate resuscitation in burns
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Urine output 0.5 mL/kg/h (adults), 1 mL/kg/h (children)
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P6
- ask: Topical agent that penetrates eschar / causes metabolic acidosis
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Mafenide acetate
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P7
- ask: Topical agent causing neutropenia or sulfa allergy issues; silver nitrate side effects
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Silver sulfadiazine (leucopenia); silver nitrate (hyponatraemia, black stain)
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P8
- ask: Investigation of choice for inhalation injury; management of CO poisoning
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Fibreoptic bronchoscopy; 100% oxygen; pulse oximetry unreliable
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P9
- ask: Indication and site of escharotomy
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Circumferential full-thickness burn of limb or chest; midlateral/midaxial lines
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P10
- ask: Curling's ulcer; Marjolin's ulcer; zones of Jackson
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Curling's = duodenal stress ulcer in burns; Marjolin = SCC in burn scar; coagulation, stasis, hyperaemia
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P11
- ask: Burn depth features (painless leathery = full thickness; blisters = partial)
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Full thickness is painless and does not blanch
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P12
- ask: Electrical burn fluid management and myoglobinuria; succinylcholine in burns
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Higher urine output target; avoid succinylcholine after 24-48 h
- seen_at: model knowledge
