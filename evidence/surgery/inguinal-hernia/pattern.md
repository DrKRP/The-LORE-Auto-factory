# Exam pattern — Inguinal hernia (surgery)

Model-knowledge entries. They steer coverage and the trap brief; they never support a fact.

## P1
- ask: Boundaries of Hesselbach's triangle
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Inferior epigastric artery (lateral), lateral border of rectus sheath (medial), inguinal ligament (inferior)
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P2
- ask: Relation of direct vs indirect inguinal hernia to the inferior epigastric vessels
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Indirect lateral, direct medial
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P3
- ask: Most common hernia in females / in children
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Indirect inguinal hernia
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P4
- ask: Boundaries / walls of the inguinal canal (posterior wall, conjoint tendon, floor)
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Posterior wall transversalis fascia with conjoint tendon medially; floor inguinal ligament
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P5
- ask: Surface marking of the deep inguinal ring
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: 1.25 cm above the midinguinal point
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P6
- ask: Sliding hernia — which viscus forms the wall of the sac on the left side
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Sigmoid colon
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P7
- ask: Named hernias — Littre's, Amyand's, de Garengeot, Richter's, Maydl's
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Meckel's diverticulum; appendix in inguinal sac; appendix in femoral sac; part of bowel wall; W-loop
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P8
- ask: Treatment of inguinal hernia in a child
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Herniotomy
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P9
- ask: Triangle of doom contents / boundaries in laparoscopic repair
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Vas medially, gonadal vessels laterally; contains external iliac vessels
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P10
- ask: Nerve most commonly injured in open inguinal hernia repair
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Ilioinguinal nerve
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P11
- ask: Preferred approach for bilateral or recurrent inguinal hernia
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Laparoscopic (TEP/TAPP)
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P12
- ask: Clinical test to differentiate direct from indirect hernia (deep ring occlusion test, Zieman's test)
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Deep ring occlusion test
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P13
- ask: Bilateral inguinal hernia in a phenotypic female — suspect which condition
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Androgen insensitivity syndrome (testicular feminisation)
- seen_at: model knowledge
