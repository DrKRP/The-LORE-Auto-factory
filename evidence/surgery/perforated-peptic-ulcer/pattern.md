# Exam pattern — Perforated peptic ulcer (surgery)

Knowledge mode: entries come from the writer's knowledge of NEET PG / INI-CET asks, not from fetched recall pages. They steer coverage and ⚠ Exam notes only; they never support a fact.

## P1
- ask: Most common site of peptic ulcer perforation
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Anterior wall of the first part of the duodenum
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P2
- ask: Investigation of choice / first investigation for suspected perforation (erect chest X-ray vs CT)
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Erect chest X-ray first (free gas under diaphragm); CT abdomen most sensitive
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P3
- ask: Surgical treatment of perforated duodenal ulcer
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Omental patch closure (Graham's patch) with peritoneal lavage
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P4
- ask: Which duodenal ulcer bleeds and which perforates; artery involved in bleeding posterior DU
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Posterior DU bleeds (gastroduodenal artery); anterior DU perforates
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P5
- ask: Most common cause of pneumoperitoneum / free gas under diaphragm
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Perforated peptic (duodenal) ulcer
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P6
- ask: X-ray view when the patient cannot stand to show free gas
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Left lateral decubitus
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P7
- ask: Perforated ulcer fluid tracking to right iliac fossa mimicking appendicitis (eponym)
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Valentino's syndrome
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P8
- ask: Clinical signs of perforation peritonitis (board-like rigidity, obliteration of liver dullness, shoulder-tip pain)
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Board-like rigidity with obliterated liver dullness
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P9
- ask: Conservative (non-operative) management of sealed perforation — name of method
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Taylor's method
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P10
- ask: Prognostic scoring in perforated peptic ulcer
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Boey score (shock, major medical illness, delay >24 h)
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P11
- ask: Mandatory step for perforated gastric (not duodenal) ulcer
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Biopsy of ulcer edge to exclude carcinoma
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P12
- ask: Pseudo-pneumoperitoneum sign / signs of pneumoperitoneum on supine film
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Chilaiditi sign (pseudo); Rigler's, football, falciform ligament signs (true)
- seen_at: model knowledge
