# Exam pattern — Thyroid nodule and differentiated thyroid cancer

Knowledge mode: these asks come from model knowledge of NEET PG / INI-CET recall patterns, not from fetched recall sources. They steer coverage and the trap brief only.

## P1
- ask: Most common type of thyroid cancer
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Papillary carcinoma
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P2
- ask: First investigation / investigation of choice for a solitary thyroid nodule
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: FNAC (older keys); TSH + ultrasound first in current practice
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P3
- ask: Histological feature diagnostic of papillary carcinoma (Orphan Annie eye nuclei, psammoma bodies, nuclear grooves)
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Nuclear features (Orphan Annie eye / ground-glass nuclei)
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P4
- ask: Why FNAC cannot diagnose follicular carcinoma / criterion for follicular carcinoma
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Capsular and/or vascular invasion on histology
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P5
- ask: Route of spread and common metastatic sites of follicular vs papillary carcinoma
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: FTC haematogenous to lung and bone; PTC lymphatic to neck nodes
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P6
- ask: Tumour marker for follow-up after total thyroidectomy for DTC
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Serum thyroglobulin
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P7
- ask: Thyroid cancer associated with radiation exposure / in children
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Papillary carcinoma
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P8
- ask: Bethesda system category and management (e.g. Bethesda IV — follicular neoplasm → lobectomy)
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Bethesda IV → hemithyroidectomy / molecular testing
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P9
- ask: Most common complication after total thyroidectomy; nerve injury causing loss of high-pitched voice
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Hypocalcaemia; external branch of superior laryngeal nerve
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P10
- ask: "Lateral aberrant thyroid" represents
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Cervical lymph-node metastasis of papillary carcinoma
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P11
- ask: Most common mutation in papillary carcinoma
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: BRAF V600E
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P12
- ask: Treatment of choice for differentiated thyroid cancer; role of radioiodine ablation and TSH suppression
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Total thyroidectomy followed by radioiodine (risk-based) and levothyroxine suppression
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P13
- ask: Age cut-off in AJCC staging of DTC
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: 45 years (older keys); 55 years in AJCC 8th edition
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P14
- ask: Most common malignancy in a thyroglossal duct cyst
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Papillary carcinoma
- seen_at: model knowledge
