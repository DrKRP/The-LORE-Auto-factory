# Exam pattern — Acute appendicitis (surgery)

## P1
- ask: Most common position of the appendix
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Retrocaecal
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P2
- ask: Rovsing's sign / psoas sign / obturator sign — which is elicited how, and which appendix position each suggests
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Rovsing = LIF pressure causes RIF pain; psoas = hip extension (retrocaecal); obturator = internal rotation of flexed hip (pelvic)
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P3
- ask: Components and weighting of the Alvarado (MANTRELS) score
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Total 10; RIF tenderness and leucocytosis score 2 each
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P4
- ask: Management of appendicular mass
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Conservative (Ochsner–Sherren regimen), then interval appendicectomy at 6–8 weeks
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P5
- ask: Investigation of choice for acute appendicitis (adult / child / pregnancy)
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: CECT in adults; ultrasound in children and pregnancy
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P6
- ask: Blood supply of the appendix and why gangrene occurs early
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Appendicular artery (branch of ileocolic), an end artery
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P7
- ask: Most common cause of luminal obstruction in children vs adults
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Lymphoid hyperplasia (children); faecolith (adults)
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P8
- ask: Most common tumour of the appendix and when right hemicolectomy is needed
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Carcinoid; > 2 cm or base involvement → right hemicolectomy
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P9
- ask: McBurney's point surface marking / gridiron incision; nerve injured leading to inguinal hernia
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Junction of lateral 1/3 and medial 2/3 of spinoumbilical line; ilioinguinal nerve
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P10
- ask: Most common non-obstetric surgical emergency in pregnancy
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Acute appendicitis
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P11
- ask: Pylephlebitis as a complication of appendicitis
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Portal pyaemia with liver abscesses
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P12
- ask: Most common complication after appendicectomy
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Wound infection
- seen_at: model knowledge
