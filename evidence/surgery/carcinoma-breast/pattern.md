# Exam pattern — Carcinoma breast (surgery)

Knowledge mode: asks recalled from model knowledge of NEET PG / INI-CET patterns. These steer coverage and ⚠ Exam notes only; they never support a fact.

## P1
- ask: Most common site of carcinoma breast
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Upper outer quadrant
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P2
- ask: Most common histological type of breast cancer; type that is often bilateral/multicentric; type with best prognosis
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Invasive ductal carcinoma (NST); invasive lobular carcinoma; tubular carcinoma
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P3
- ask: Investigation of choice for diagnosis of a breast lump / components of triple assessment
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Core needle biopsy (older keys: FNAC); clinical exam + imaging + tissue diagnosis
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P4
- ask: TNM staging — peau d'orange, inflammatory carcinoma, fixed/matted nodes, supraclavicular nodes, pectoralis major involvement
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Peau d'orange T4b; inflammatory T4d; matted nodes N2a; supraclavicular N3c (older keys M1); pectoralis alone not T4
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P5
- ask: Most important prognostic factor in carcinoma breast
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Axillary lymph node status
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P6
- ask: Contraindications to breast-conserving surgery
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Multicentric disease, diffuse microcalcifications, prior chest RT, pregnancy needing RT, persistently positive margins, inflammatory cancer
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P7
- ask: Nerve injured in axillary dissection causing winging of scapula; nerve most commonly injured
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Long thoracic nerve (winging); intercostobrachial nerve (most commonly injured)
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P8
- ask: Hormonal therapy choice by menopausal status; side effects of tamoxifen
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Tamoxifen premenopausal, aromatase inhibitor postmenopausal; endometrial cancer, thromboembolism, cataract
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P9
- ask: Trastuzumab target and toxicity
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: HER2; cardiotoxicity (reversible)
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P10
- ask: BRCA1 / BRCA2 chromosomes; gene linked to male breast cancer
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: BRCA1 17q, BRCA2 13q; BRCA2
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P11
- ask: Types of modified radical mastectomy (Patey, Scanlon, Auchincloss) and what each removes
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Patey removes pectoralis minor; Scanlon divides it; Auchincloss preserves it
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P12
- ask: Management of inflammatory breast carcinoma
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Neoadjuvant chemotherapy → MRM → radiotherapy
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P13
- ask: Most common cancer in Indian women
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Breast cancer (older keys: carcinoma cervix)
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P14
- ask: Stewart-Treves syndrome
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Lymphangiosarcoma in post-mastectomy lymphoedematous arm
- seen_at: model knowledge
