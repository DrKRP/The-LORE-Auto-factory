# Exam pattern: Gallstones and acute cholecystitis

Knowledge mode: asks recalled from model knowledge of NEET PG / INI-CET and AIIMS / PGI-style PYQs. They steer coverage and the trap brief; they support no fact.

## P1
- ask: Most common type of gallstone
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Mixed stones
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P2
- ask: Investigation of choice for gallstones / initial investigation in suspected acute cholecystitis
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Ultrasonography
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P3
- ask: Most sensitive and specific (investigation of choice) test for acute cholecystitis
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: HIDA scan (non-visualisation of gallbladder)
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P4
- ask: Timing / treatment of choice for acute calculous cholecystitis
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Older keys: conservative then interval cholecystectomy at 6–8 weeks; newer keys: early laparoscopic cholecystectomy within 72 h
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P5
- ask: Boundaries and content of Calot's triangle
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Cystic duct, common hepatic duct, inferior surface of liver; contains cystic artery
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P6
- ask: Mirizzi syndrome — definition / mechanism of jaundice
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Stone in Hartmann's pouch / cystic duct compressing the common hepatic duct
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P7
- ask: Gallstone ileus — commonest site of obstruction and Rigler's triad
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Terminal ileum; pneumobilia, small-bowel obstruction, ectopic gallstone
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P8
- ask: Indications for cholecystectomy in asymptomatic gallstones
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Porcelain gallbladder, stones >3 cm, polyp >1 cm, sickle cell disease; older keys add diabetes mellitus
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P9
- ask: Organism in emphysematous cholecystitis and the typical patient
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Clostridium perfringens; elderly diabetic men
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P10
- ask: Acalculous cholecystitis — setting and management
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Critically ill (burns, trauma, sepsis, TPN, ICU); percutaneous cholecystostomy if unfit
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P11
- ask: Tokyo Guidelines (TG18) diagnostic criteria and severity grading of acute cholecystitis
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Definite = local + systemic signs + imaging; Grade III = organ dysfunction; Grade II = WBC >18,000, tender RUQ mass, >72 h, marked local inflammation
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P12
- ask: Murphy's sign / Boas' sign / Courvoisier's law / Saint's triad (eponym matching)
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Inspiratory arrest on RUQ palpation; hyperaesthesia below right scapula; palpable GB with jaundice unlikely stones; gallstones + hiatus hernia + diverticulosis
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P13
- ask: Percentage of gallstones that are radio-opaque
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: About 10–15%
- seen_at: model knowledge

## P14
- ask: Critical view of safety in laparoscopic cholecystectomy
- frequency: model knowledge
- exam_answer: Cleared hepatocystic triangle; lower third of GB off the cystic plate; two and only two structures entering the GB
- seen_at: model knowledge
